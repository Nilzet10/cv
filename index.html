FAQ

Good to know

Can I book just one service?

Of course. Henna, the tea cart and the DJ can each be booked on their own. Bundling just means one contact and one booking for everything.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, three to six months ahead is ideal. Wedding season, Eid and Chand Raat fill up quickly, so reach out as soon as you have a date.

Do you travel to my venue?

Yes. We're based in Iselin, NJ and work events across New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania and Connecticut. A travel fee may apply for longer distances.

How long does bridal henna take?

Usually three to six hours, depending on how far up the arms and legs the design goes. Plan to relax and let the paste dry — we'll share aftercare tips so the stain comes out dark.

What do you need from the venue?

For the DJ and tea cart, a nearby power outlet and a little floor space. We'll confirm the details with your venue coordinator before the day.
